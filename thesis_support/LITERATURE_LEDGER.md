# LITERATURE LEDGER

Status values: **VERIFIED** (metadata confirmed against the CrossRef API, `api.crossref.org`) ·
**PENDING** (candidate found, metadata not yet confirmed) · **REJECTED**.

**Rule: no citation enters `references.bib` until it is VERIFIED.** Verification means the DOI
resolves and the returned authors / title / year / venue / volume / pages match the entry
exactly. Search-engine snippets are never sufficient on their own.

Books and standard textbooks are verified against publisher metadata rather than CrossRef and
are listed separately in §2.
